01 — About

A small Norfolk Street salon, kept quiet on purpose.

Modelling Hairstyles has stood in the same place on Norfolk Street since 2007. The shop is small by design — there is room for the chair, the mirror, and the time it takes to do the work properly. People come for a cut and a finish; they tend to come back for the same reason.

The point is not novelty. The point is that you sit down, you tell us what you want, and you leave with what you came for. Children are welcome and treated gently. Repeat customers are remembered. The kettle is on.

02 — In the chair

Honest hairdressing, without the spa-menu language.

i.Cutting

Ladies', gents' and children's cuts. A consultation first — what grows where, what behaves, what you actually want when you wake up on Wednesday.

ii.Colour & highlights

Full colour, root retouch, highlights and lowlights. Skin tests for first-time clients as a matter of course. Bring a reference photo if you have one.

iii.Blow-dry & finish

Wash, blow-dry and finish for everyday or for an evening out. Twenty minutes longer than you think is the time it takes to do it well.

iv.Children's hair

First cuts and regular trims, calmly. We work at the child's pace, not the clock's. Parents are welcome to sit alongside.

v.Restyling

If you have decided the time has come for a change, sit down for a proper conversation. We will not rush you, and we will say so if your idea will not suit.

Prices are quoted in the chair before the work begins — every head is different, and we would rather agree the cost with you in person than print a guess on a website. Call 01553 774711 for an estimate or to book.
